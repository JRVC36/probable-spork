What happens next

  1. Submit a registration request

    Choose a session and your payment preference. Family Camp uses one reservation and one $100 deposit per family. Submission alone does not reserve a place.

  2. Staff confirms availability

    We respond within two business days with your amount due, applicable discounts, and payment instructions. An automatic acknowledgment is not confirmed enrollment; health forms may accompany that acknowledgment.

  3. Pay through Zeffy

    After staff confirms space, pay the $100 deposit or full amount. The deposit is applied to tuition; any balance is due two weeks before your stay begins.

  4. Receive enrollment confirmation

    Staff matches your payment to your request and confirms enrollment and any remaining balance. A Zeffy receipt alone does not confirm enrollment.

Sunday check-in is 3:00–4:30 p.m.; Saturday checkout is 9:00–10:00 a.m. Wednesday checkout is at 10:00 a.m. and check-in is at 4:00 p.m.

Pay for camp through Zeffy

First submit your registration request and wait for staff to confirm space and payment instructions. If your reservation is already confirmed, use the appropriate link below.

Pay the $100 deposit (opens in a new tab) Pay a balance or full tuition (opens in a new tab)

Family Camp: buy one $100 deposit for the whole family reservation. Your deposit is credited toward tuition; do not pay it again when settling your balance.

For balance or full payment, choose “Summer Camp Balance” and enter the exact amount provided by JRVC, including approved discounts, camperships, and previous payments. “Pay what you can” is Zeffy’s amount-entry label here, not optional tuition. Contact staff if you do not know your amount.

Use the same contact email as your registration. Include participant name and session wherever the checkout requests them; if there is no place to add them, email those details to kevin@jrvc.org so staff can match the payment.

Each payment is a separate checkout. There are no automatic future charges. A Zeffy receipt confirms a transaction, not enrollment or a zero balance. JRVC will confirm payment matching and any remaining balance. These links are not for general donations.

Cost is a concern?
