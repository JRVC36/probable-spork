Register for camp.

One form per camper. A $100 deposit holds the spot, and we confirm by email within two business days.

Camper registration

Fields marked * are required.

Which session?

For Adult Camp or Family Camp, please include your preferred three-night or full-week stay in the notes below. For Adult Camp, also specify single or double occupancy. For Family Camp, list accompanying family members and their ages.

Parent or guardian

Anything our health officer and kitchen should know before arrival. Medical forms are sent after registration.

No payment is collected on this page. Within two business days we will email your confirmation and instructions for the $100 deposit that holds your camper’s spot.

What happens next

  1. You submit this form

    A $100 deposit reserves the spot. The balance is due two weeks before your session.

  2. We confirm by email

    Immediately upon submission, with the Camper Health History Form and HealthCare Recommendations attached.

  3. You arrive Sunday

    Check-in is 3:00–4:30 p.m. Bring the medical form; leave the phone if you can.

Cost is a concern?

Please still register — or call first. The Campership Fund covers part or all of tuition, and asking is confidential. Call 828-387-5736 or email kevin@jrvc.org.

Prefer paper?

We will mail or email a PDF registration form. Call the camp office at 828-754-7067.

Your information is used only to run your camper’s registration. See our privacy policy.
