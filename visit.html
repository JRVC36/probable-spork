Collettsville, North Carolina

Getting here.

The camp is 20 minutes off NC-90 in Caldwell County, about 30 minutes from Lenoir and 90 minutes from Charlotte. The last few miles are rural — download directions before you lose signal.

Directions

6211 NC-90, Collettsville, NC 28611

From Charlotte (approx. 1 hr 45 min): take I-85 North to US-321 North toward Hickory and Lenoir. At Lenoir, continue north on NC-90 through Collettsville. The camp entrance is on the right, about 6 miles past the Collettsville post office.

From Asheville (approx. 1 hr 45 min): take I-40 East to NC-181 North toward Pineola, then NC-90 East toward Collettsville. Watch for the camp sign on the left.

From Boone (approx. 1 hr): take US-321 South to Blowing Rock, then NC-181 South, then NC-90 East.

Before you drive in

Cell service drops for the last 10–15 minutes of the drive, and there is no reliable GPS once you are on NC-90 in the valley. Open the map link below and start navigation before you leave the highway. If you get lost, the camp office phone still reaches a landline: 828-754-7067.

The road in is paved, but the last stretch is narrow and winding. Carpool if you can. Parking is available outside the lodge and near the cabins — do not drive onto the grass.

Two addresses

Mail goes to Kannapolis. People go to Collettsville. Please do not drive to the Kannapolis address — it is the administrative office, not the camp.
