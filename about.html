Since 1936

Ninety years in the same valley.

Johns River Valley Camp is the outdoor ministry site of the Southern Conference, United Church of Christ. The buildings have changed. The river has not.

Our mission

Deep connections to God, real growth, and a community that holds.

Johns River Valley Camp is dedicated to creating transformative experiences in a stunning natural setting. Here, we nurture deep connections to God, inspire personal growth, and cultivate strong, supportive community.

Since 1936, JRVC has welcomed children, youth, adults, families, church groups, and organizations year-round. Generations of the same families still come, and the reason they give is almost always the same: something happened here that did not happen anywhere else.

A place like no other

The site

Nestled in the foothills of North Carolina’s Blue Ridge Mountains, JRVC rests beside a mountain stream on 150 acres, with access to waterfalls, hiking trails, an archery course, river activities, a climbing wall, a low ropes course, a basketball court, and two outdoor chapels.

The main site sits on a slope above the Johns River. The dining hall and lodge anchor the middle of it, cabins and bathhouses spread along the ridge, and the primitive Outpost sits furthest out — close enough to walk to the bathhouse, far enough to see the stars.

What we believe

Statement of Faith

Our camp is rooted in the Christian faith and the belief that God’s love invites us into a life of discovery, compassion, and community.

We Believe in God

We believe in one God—Father, Son, and Holy Spirit—who creates and sustains life. Every person is made in God’s image, is deeply loved, and is worthy of kindness, dignity, and care.

We Follow Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, our Savior, whose life, teachings, death, and resurrection reveal God’s forgiving and renewing love. We seek to follow Jesus by loving our neighbors, serving others, practicing forgiveness, and living with compassion.

We Trust the Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit is present and active among us, offering comfort, guidance, and strength. The Spirit helps us grow in faith and draws us together in community.

We Learn Through Scripture and Faith

We receive the Bible as a sacred witness to God’s work and a guide for faith and life. We approach Scripture with prayer, thoughtful reflection, and openness to deeper understanding. Honest questions and continued learning are welcome parts of a growing faith.

We Live in Grace and Community

We believe God’s grace is a gift, not something we must earn. We seek to reflect that grace by encouraging one another, making room for differences, and extending forgiveness when we fall short.

We Put Faith into Practice

At camp, we seek to make God’s love visible through friendship, worship, service, play, and care for creation. We encourage each camper to discover their gifts and use them to help others.

We Welcome All

Our Christian faith shapes our camp, and our welcome extends to campers and families of all backgrounds and faith traditions. Whether faith is familiar, new, uncertain, or still being explored, we welcome your questions, your curiosity, and your unique gifts.

Wherever you are on your journey, you are welcome here.
