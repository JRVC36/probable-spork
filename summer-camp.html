Adult Camp

June 13–19, 2027

Who says camp is just for kids?

Rediscover the joy of summer camp—with the freedom to enjoy it your way. Join in scheduled activities, share laughter and conversation, or settle beside the river with a good book. Whether you’re looking for adventure, connection, or a little breathing room, Adult Camp offers traditional camp fun at your own pace. Come ready to play, rest, and make room for joy.

Drama Camp

June 27–July 3, 2027

Find your voice. Bring a story to life.

Join fellow campers for a week of imagination, rehearsals, and teamwork as you create a production together. Whether you love the spotlight or are stepping onto the stage for the first time, you’ll have opportunities to build confidence, stretch your creativity, and discover the joy of making something bigger than yourself. The week culminates in a Saturday-morning performance for families at pickup—a chance to share the story and celebrate everything you’ve created.

Children and the adults who call them family

Family Camp

July 4–10, 2027

Your family. Your adventure. Time together that matters.

Bring the children in your life to JRVC for a week with room for both togetherness and independence. Parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, guardians, foster families, and multigenerational groups are welcome—children and the adults who call them family. Enjoy activities as a family, let kids connect with other kids, and take advantage of time just for adults. With opportunities to choose your own adventure, you can find the balance of exploration, play, and rest that fits your family. Make new friends, share in the camp community, and head home with stories you’ll keep telling long after summer ends.

Rates are per person, per night and include lodging, meals, and camp activities. Adults (18+): $75; teens (12–17): $50; children (4–11): $30; children 3 and under: free.

A typical day

Structure, with room to get muddy.

The day has a shape so campers know what comes next — and enough open space that it still feels like summer.

  1. 8:30 — Morning rally

    Songs, the day’s goals, and a head count before anything else happens.

  2. 10:00 — Adventure block

    Outdoor sports, nature hikes, archery, or a scavenger hunt depending on the group.

  3. 12:00 — Lunch & rest

    A real meal in the dining hall, then quiet shade time for reading or journaling.

  4. 1:30 — Choice electives

    Campers pick between art, STEM, or field games. Choosing is part of the fun.

  5. 4:30 — Farewell circle

    Reflections, awards, and packing up before pickup.

Also for older campers

Counselor in Training

A leadership program for 16-year-olds who are done being campers but not done with camp.

CITs work alongside counselors for the full length of a session, learning group management, activity leadership, and how a camp day actually runs. It is unpaid, and room and board are provided.

  • Minimum age 16 at the start of the session
  • At least two years older than the campers in their group
  • One reference from a teacher, mentor, or former camp staff member
  • Full session commitment, including training and feedback meetings

Summer staff opportunities

We hire four to six resident leaders each summer, plus activity specialists in archery, lifeguarding, climbing, and paddling. Housing and meals included.
