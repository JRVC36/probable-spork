Lodging

Four ways to sleep here.

Groups choose based on how much comfort they want and how much they want to rough it. Rates below are per person, per night, and include use of all meeting and program space.

Check availability Retreat rates

Sleeps 20–24 per building · $78 /person /night

Boger Shuford Living Units

Two modern, fully accessible buildings, each with six adult-friendly bedrooms and a private bath in every room. Four rooms hold a full bed plus a bunk; two hold two sets of bunks. Each building has its own kitchen, dining area, and central heat and air.

Sleeps 12 · $72 /person /night

Mt. Zion Cabin

A self-contained cabin in the main site, available on its own without booking the whole property. Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen, and a central common area — the easiest option for a small group or a family gathering.

Sleeps 8–14 per cabin · $62 /person /night

Main Site

Nine traditional cabins without bathrooms, served by two separate bathhouses. The main site also includes the kitchen and dining hall, a recreation building, twelve camper hook-ups, and two outdoor chapels. The bathhouses, dining hall, recreation room, and Mt. Zion Cabin are accessible.

Sleeps up to 40 · $48 /person /night

Outpost

Four A-frame cabins, an activity shelter, a worship area, a covered cooking area, and latrines. Deliberately primitive: no drinking water and no electricity. The right choice for groups who want the quiet and the stars and mean it.

Outpost has no running water or power. Groups staying there use the main site bathhouses, a five-minute walk away.

Meeting space

Boger Abernethy Bridge

A 900-square-foot program room with tables, chairs, a projector, and central heat and air. There is a small kitchen and a private bath in the same unit, so a group can meet, eat, and break out without moving buildings.

Included with lodging at no additional charge — no facility fee, no hourly rate.
