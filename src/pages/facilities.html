Lodging & camping

Ways to stay here.

Groups and families choose based on how much comfort they want and how much they want to rough it. Rates below are flat nightly rates per room, cabin, or campsite.

Private bath in each room · $80 UCC / $95 Non-UCC per room /night

Lodge Rooms (Boger Shuford Units)

Two modern, fully accessible buildings, each with six adult-friendly bedrooms and a private bath in every room. $80/night per room for members of United Church of Christ congregations, or $95/night per room for Non-UCC guests. Each building has its own kitchen, dining area, and central heat and air.

3 BR · 2 BA · Sleeps up to 12 · $120 /night flat rate

Mt. Zion Cabin

Flat rate of $120 per night for the entire unit. Ideal for larger families or groups — three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a kitchen, and a central common area, sleeping up to 12 in bunk beds.

Sleeps 8–10 per cabin · $75 /cabin /night flat rate

Standard Cabins (Main Site)

Flat rate of $75 per night per cabin. Traditional cabins sleeping up to 8–10 guests in bunk beds, served by two accessible bathhouses on the main site.

$18 /tent · $25 /RV site per night

Camping & RV Sites

Tent sites are $18 per night per tent with access to shared bathhouse facilities. Twelve Camper/RV sites are $25 per night per site and include water and electric hookups.

We also maintain a primitive Outpost (four A-frame cabins, activity shelter, worship area, covered cooking area, and latrines) for groups looking for an unplugged backcountry experience.

Facility rentals

Meeting, dining & worship spaces.

The Bridge Unit (Boger Abernethy Bridge) is a 900-square-foot versatile meeting space with tables, chairs, a projector, a small kitchen, a private bath, and central heat and air — ideal for workshops or gatherings at $135 per day.

Groups can also rent the main Dining Hall & Kitchen ($160 per day) and the Outdoor Chapel ($500 per day, ideal for worship services, weddings, or vow renewals).
