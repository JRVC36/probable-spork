Adult Camp

03 / SUMMER 2027

Rising grades 6–12 and graduating seniors

Drama Camp

June 27–July 3, 2027

$560per camper / session

Find your voice. Bring a story to life.

Join fellow campers for a week of imagination, rehearsals, and teamwork as you create a production together. Whether you love the spotlight or are stepping onto the stage for the first time, you’ll have opportunities to build confidence, stretch your creativity, and discover the joy of making something bigger than yourself. 04 / SUMMER 2027

Children and the adults who call them family

Family Camp

July 4–10, 2027

Your family. Your adventure. Time together that matters.

Bring the children in your life to JRVC for a week with room for both togetherness and independence. Parents, grandparents, aunts, uncles, guardians, foster families, and multigenerational groups are welcome—children and the adults who call them family. Enjoy activities as a family, let kids connect with other kids, and take advantage of time just for adults. With opportunities to choose your own adventure, you can find the balance of exploration, play, and rest that fits your family. Make new friends, share in the camp community, and head home with stories you’ll keep telling long after summer ends.

Also for older campers

Counselor in Training

A leadership program for 16-year-olds who are done being campers but not done with camp.

CITs work alongside counselors for the full length of a session, learning group management, activity leadership, and how a camp day actually runs. It is unpaid, and room and board are provided.

  • Minimum age 16 at the start of the session
  • At least two years older than the campers in their group
  • One reference from a teacher, mentor, or former camp staff member
  • Full session commitment, including training and feedback meetings

Summer staff opportunities

We hire four to six resident leaders each summer, plus activity specialists in archery, lifeguarding, climbing, and paddling. Housing and meals included.
