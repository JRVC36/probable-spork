Youth groups & college teams

Your mission trip, handled.

Lodging, meals, vetted service projects, and a real day off — all in one price. You bring the students and the energy.

What a week looks like

Real work, real people, and time to process it.

Groups serve alongside established local ministries in the Southern Appalachian Mountains — building ramps for elderly neighbors, staffing food pantries, running children’s day camps, and cleaning up streams and roadsides.

The work is arranged before you arrive. Nobody spends Monday morning on the phone looking for a project.

Evenings are for group devotions and debriefing, because the point is not the ramp. It is what students notice while building it.

  • Vetted projects arranged with local partners before your group arrives
  • All meals in the dining hall, plus packed lunches on work days
  • Tools, materials, and a project leader on site each day
  • A Wednesday off for waterfalls, canoeing, or tubing the Johns River
  • Guided evening devotions and space for your own program

Choose the work your group cares about.

Theme 01

Rural Appalachia

The most requested theme. Groups work on home repair for elderly and low-income neighbors who cannot manage it themselves.

  • Wheelchair ramp construction
  • Roofing and small home repairs
  • Yard cleanup and weatherizing

Theme 02

Unhoused Assistance

Partnership with local ministries serving people experiencing homelessness and food insecurity across the county.

  • Cooking and serving meals
  • Sorting donations at a thrift store
  • Stocking and staffing a food pantry

Theme 03

Environmental Justice

Hands-on stewardship of the watershed your group is living in for the week, with the Johns River as the classroom.

  • Paddle-and-pick-up river cleanup
  • Roadside and trail clearing
  • Stream bank stabilization
