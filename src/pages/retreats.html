Who comes

Church groups, staff teams, family reunions, and everybody in between.

JRVC sits on 150 acres beside the Johns River, 20 minutes off NC-90 in Collettsville. The site is quiet enough to think in and big enough to spread out. Groups come for staff planning retreats, youth lock-ins, women’s and men’s weekends, family reunions, quilting retreats, weddings, and memorials.

We handle lodging, meals, and meeting space. You bring your people and whatever you came to do.

  • Three meals a day in the dining hall, cooked on site by our kitchen staff
  • Meeting space with tables, chairs, a projector, and central heat and air
  • Lodging for 8 to 120, from modern accessible units to primitive outpost cabins

Questions groups ask

Before you commit.

Can we cook for ourselves?

Yes. The Boger Shuford units and Mt. Zion Cabin have full kitchens. You can take meals in the dining hall, cook in your unit, or mix the two — tell us which meals you want and we will bill accordingly.

What is the site like for someone with mobility needs?

The Boger Shuford units, Mt. Zion Cabin, both bathhouses, the dining hall, and the recreation room are accessible. The main site is on a slope with paved paths between the principal buildings. Tell us at booking and we will place your group in the most accessible lodging.

Is there internet and cell service?

The meeting spaces have wireless internet and the lodge has good coverage. Cell reception is limited on parts of the property and in the valley. If your group needs reliable connectivity, tell us and we will place you in the building with the strongest signal.

Can we hold a wedding or memorial service?

Yes, and the outdoor chapels are the reason many people do. We host weddings, memorials, family reunions, and retirement parties. Tell us what you are planning in the inquiry form and we will walk through space, seating, and catering.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made more than 45 days before arrival are refunded in full, including the deposit. Inside 45 days, the deposit is applied to a future booking within 12 months. Final head count is due two weeks out.
