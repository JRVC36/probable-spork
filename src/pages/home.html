Why JRVC

We take the screens away and hand back the day.

Our mission is simple: help youth seek God, build nurturing relationships, and rediscover the joy of the outdoors. By stepping away from digital distractions, campers find the space to listen — to nature, to their peers, and to their Creator.

Campers sleep in cabins, eat in the dining hall, and spend the day at archery, on the climbing wall, in a canoe, on the trail, or sitting still in the chapel at the top of the hill.

  • Grouped by developmental stage — never too easy, never overwhelming.
  • A squad system so every camper is known by name and included.
  • Staff who are trained, screened, and here all summer — not passing through.

All questions
What does a week at resident camp cost?

Contact us for 2027 tuition, registration details, and campership information. Explore the new summer schedule to find the right camp for you or your family.

How are staff screened and trained?

Every staff member completes a background check, references, and a pre-camp training week covering supervision, safety, and emergency procedure. See our safety and staffing page for ratios and health coverage.

What time is drop-off and pick-up?

Drop-off is Sunday between 3:00 and 4:30 p.m. Pick-up is Saturday between 9:00 and 10:00 a.m. Day camp runs 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.

My camper has allergies or takes medication. Who handles that?

The health form is part of registration and goes to the camp health officer before arrival. Medication is collected and stored at check-in and dispensed by staff. The kitchen accommodates dietary needs with advance notice — tell us when you register.

Can we afford camp?

Yes, if we can help. The Campership Fund exists to remove cost as a barrier, and asking is confidential and normal. Email kevin@jrvc.org or call 828-387-5736.
