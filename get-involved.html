Give · Volunteer · Work here

Camp runs on people who care.

Some give money. Some give a Saturday. Some give a summer. All of it keeps ninety years of this place going.

Why it matters

A week at camp changes what a kid believes about themselves.

Camp is where confidence is found, resilience is built, and friendships get made face to face instead of over a screen. In a world full of screens and schedules, it is a vital pause. That does not happen on its own — it is powered by people who believe in it.

Volunteer

Current projects on site.

Work weekends run spring through fall, usually Friday evening through Saturday afternoon. All skill levels welcome — we will teach you what you need to know, and lunch is provided.

Two phases · Interior

Women’s Bathhouse Renovation

Phase one: remove the tile and stalls. Phase two: install new stall dividers for showers and toilets and paint and finish the interior walls. Best suited to groups of 6–10; no trade experience needed for demo and painting.

Two phases · Interior

Men’s Bathhouse Renovation

The same scope as the women’s bathhouse, run in parallel. Demo, then new partitions, fixtures, and paint. A good project for a church group that wants visible progress in a single Saturday.

Carpentry · Outdoor

Vehicle Shelter

Build an enclosed pole-barn shelter for the camp vehicles, which currently sit out in the weather year-round. Framing and roofing experience welcome but not required — we will pair you with someone who has it.

Four phases · New build

New Cabin

Framing, then dry-in with siding and roofing, then mechanical and utilities, then interior finishing. The biggest project on the list and the one that will take the most weekends — and the most skilled hands.

Bring your group for a work weekend

Church groups, scout troops, campus ministries, and corporate teams are all welcome. We can host up to 60 volunteers with lodging and meals, and we will scope a project to fit your group’s size and skill level.

Work here

Summer staff 2026

We hire four to six full-time resident leaders each summer, plus activity specialists. It is hard work, it pays modestly, and people come back for years.

  • Housing and all meals included for the season
  • Pre-camp training week covering supervision, safety, and programming
  • Positions in archery, lifeguarding, climbing, canoeing, music, arts and crafts, and low ropes
  • Background check and references required for all staff
